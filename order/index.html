Pricing Reference

Custom cookies add an extra special touch to the moments that become memories that last a lifetime. Each order is designed, baked, and hand decorated personally for you and your loved ones.

Pricing starts at $78/dozen and varies based on design complexity and color palette. A 2-dozen minimum applies to all tiers, with the exception of the “My Favorite Things” set which has a 1-dozen minimum. All cookies are vanilla sugar cookies with royal icing, 3–4 inches in size, and individually heat sealed for freshness. Cookie flavor upgrades are available — just note your preference on the order form.

Classic Set
$78+/doz

Includes up to 4 designs with up to 3 colors (not including white). Clean, beautifully decorated cookies that are anything but basic — perfect for everyday celebrations and last-minute gifting.

Signature Set
$84+/doz

Includes up to 6 designs with up to 5 colors (not including white). This tier is perfect for orders featuring complex elements like 3D florals, metallic accents (gold, silver, or rose gold), lettering, or multi-layer icing work.

Showstopper
$96+/doz

Includes up to 8 designs with up to 6 colors (not including white). This tier is reserved for the most intricate work — think elaborate 3D florals, metallic accents, hand painted details, and highly detailed decorating that makes every cookie a centerpiece.

My Favorite Things
$108+/doz

Looking for a truly thoughtful gift? This set is designed to celebrate someone special in the most personal way. Each set includes 12 cookies, every single one uniquely decorated to reflect that person’s interests, hobbies, and favorite things. Comes beautifully packaged with ribbon, bows, or a custom tag — completely gift-ready. Share as many details as possible about the 12 favorite things you’d like represented. The more you tell us, the more personal your set will be!

Character Cookies
$8–$10 each

A premium add-on to any cookie set. Each cookie features one intricately hand-decorated character design, priced individually due to the detailed artistry and time required. Available as an add-on only — not sold as a standalone order.
